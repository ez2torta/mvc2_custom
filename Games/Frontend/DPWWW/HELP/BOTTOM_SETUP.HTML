7. Using the Bookmarklets Section

If you click the "BOOKMARK" tab in the top menu bar within XDP, you will be presented with the Bookmarklets page. Some of these can be used directly from that page, but others need to be added as a bookmark. Once a bookmark is added, it can be used at any time while you surf the web.

  1. To save a bookmark, press "copy" next to the bookmarklet you want to save. Once the text in the textbox is highlighted, either press "copy" from the onscreen keyboard or press Ctrl+C on your hardware keyboard.
  2. Open your bookmarks from the L-Trigger menu.
  3. Open the R-Trigger menu and select "ADD NEW" to add a new bookmark.
  4. Enter a name for your bookmark and paste the bookmarklet as the URL by selecting "paste" from the onscreen keyboard or pressing Ctrl+V on your hardware keyboard.
  5. Select to "Save" from the R-Trigger menu and save to your VMU.
There is also a URL bar at the top of the bookmarklets page that you can use to jump to any internet site.

Note: It is recommended that you save the Notepad bookmarklet to your VMU. It can come in handy as you surf the web.
